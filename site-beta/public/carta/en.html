The closed beta on Google Play is open for you. You are among the first to hatch yours.

How to install

  1. Using the same Gmail account you use on Google Play, join our Google Group
  2. Open the test link on your Android phone and tap Become a tester
  3. Install Soulmon from the Play Store. No developer mode, nothing to download outside the store

For ages 18 and up, on an Android phone. If Play says the app is not available, wait a few minutes after becoming a tester and try again.
