# The Investor Bar (YC / McKinsey)

The rubric every artifact is graded against. A product is **high-ticket-ready** when each
dimension is 🟢 with evidence. Grade 🟢 (evidenced) / 🟡 (plausible, unproven) / 🔴 (missing or weak).

| # | Dimension | What 🟢 looks like | Classic 🔴 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Problem & insight** | A sharp, real pain + a non-obvious insight into why it persists | "Everyone has this problem" |
| 2 | **Market & timing** | Bottom-up TAM that reconciles top-down; a concrete "why now" | "$Xbn market, we take 1%" |
| 3 | **Product & wedge** | MVP isolates the riskiest assumption; a clear initial wedge | A mini version of everything |
| 4 | **Unit economics** | Credible path to LTV/CAC > 3, payback < 12–18mo | CAC hidden behind "viral" |
| 5 | **Moat & defensibility** | An advantage that compounds (data / network / switching / distribution) | "We'll execute faster" |
| 6 | **Execution credibility** | Architecture, quality, and design that prove this team can build it | Pretty deck, no substance |
| 7 | **Narrative clarity** | One-line thesis + the 3 numbers that matter | A feature list |
| 8 | **Evidence over assertion** | Every claim sourced or tested; assumptions labeled | Confident unsourced numbers |

## How to use

- Each persona self-grades its own outputs on the dimensions it owns (see each agent's `## Investor Bar`).
- The orchestrator aggregates the grade at every checkpoint.
- The bar is **diagnostic, not decorative**: a 🔴 is the most valuable signal in the room — it
  tells you exactly what a YC partner will attack first. Fix it or own it out loud.

## The two graders to imagine

- **The YC partner:** *"Why you, why now, why won't an incumbent do this? Show me users who'd cry if it vanished."*
- **The McKinsey EM:** *"Walk me through the math. Where's that number from? What single assumption breaks the model?"*
